# League Hub Future Ideas

This document tracks potential enhancements for the League Hub and Fixtures system.

## 1. Match Scorers in Fixture List
*   **Concept:** Display individual goalscorers underneath the final score in the Fixtures & Results tab.
*   **Technical Need:** Update the `Fixture` interface to store an array of `goalEvents` (playerId and minute).
*   **Impact:** Adds narrative depth to the league, allowing players to see which rival stars are performing in specific matches.

## 2. League News Feed
*   **Concept:** A dynamic "News" or "Social" feed on the League Hub dashboard.
*   **Content:** Auto-generated headlines for big wins (4+ goals), hat-tricks, or top-of-the-table clashes.
*   **Impact:** Makes the league feel more "alive" and reactive to match results.

## 3. Golden Boot Race Preview
*   **Concept:** Feature the current league top scorer prominently on the main Standings tab.
*   **Content:** "Player to Watch" or "League Leader" card showing the striker's name, team, and current goal tally.
*   **Impact:** Enhances the competitive feeling of individual player stats within the team-focused league view.
